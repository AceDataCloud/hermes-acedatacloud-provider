# Brand assets

`logo.png` is the unmodified official Ace Data Cloud logo from
https://cdn.acedata.cloud/assets/logo.png (512 × 512 RGBA, 60,479 bytes).
SHA-256: `3e2c6e2d1a61a549dae2374ec4444c77f4c2617c8f3772bdd4cd6e52bf20767e`.

`catalog.png` places that complete image, proportionally resized to 280 × 280,
at (460, 160) on a transparent 1200 × 600 canvas. The extra transparent space
fits Hermes's 2:1 catalog cards and keeps the complete logo inside the detail
page's center crop. No border, background, crop or recoloring is applied to
the source artwork. Hermes supplies the surrounding card styling.
